What the data says

Vanguard's analysis was among the sharpest in the room — Analytical Rigor of 82 sat comfortably above the cohort median. The story of their quarter is not whether they could do the work, but two habits that quietly cost them.

First, an early data-trust slip. The Round-1 formatting miss meant the opening revenue figure was never fully clean, and because later rounds build on that number, Data Trust never fully recovered — it finished at 68 despite strong reconciliation in Round 3. In this simulation, an early miss follows the team all the way to the Board.

Second, pace under pressure. Turnaround Discipline (60) was the team's clear growth area — they reached good answers, but slower than the cohort when the clock was shortest. Their redeeming moment was Round 2: when new information broke, they revised quickly and confidently — exactly the calibration a Board wants to see.

Strengths

  • High analytical rigor — the underlying work was consistently correct.
  • Excellent recovery under new information (Round 2 revision).
  • Clear, well-chosen data visualization.

Development pointers

  • Validate formatting before publishing a headline number — early trust compounds.
  • Move decisively when the clock is short; a defensible answer on time beats a perfect one late.
  • Lead the Board synthesis with the recommendation, then the evidence.
